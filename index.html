Welcome

My name is Tsz Ching Fan (樊子晴 faan4 zi2 cing4). I am a second-year MRes/PhD student in Economics at the University of Warwick, under the supervision of Prof. Daniel Sgroi. I hold degrees of MSc in Economics from the London School of Economics and Political Science and BSSc in Economics from the Chinese University of Hong Kong.

I work on behavioural economics, with applications to labour, development, and wellbeing. I am a member of the Experimental and Behavioural Economics Research Group and the Applied Microeconomics Research Group. My current research explores endogenous discounting.

You can contact me at tsz-ching.fan@warwick.ac.uk.
